About Renegade Air

Kenya's Most Adventurous Airline

Renegade Air was founded with a simple mission: provide safe, dependable, and cost-effective air transport to every corner of Kenya. From the Maasai Mara to the shores of Lamu, we open the country up to travellers who refuse to be confined by distance.

Our modern turboprop fleet is maintained to the highest KCAA standards, piloted by seasoned aviators with deep knowledge of Kenya's diverse terrain and weather patterns.
